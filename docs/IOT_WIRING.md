# SafeRoute Saheli — IoT Hardware Wiring & Schematics Guide

This document provides complete, production-grade electrical wiring diagrams, component ratings, pull-up/pull-down resistor specifications, and safety precautions for both hardware devices in the SafeRoute Saheli ecosystem:
1. **ESP32 Wearable Safety Device** (All-in-one safety band / pendant)
2. **Dedicated ESP32-CAM Optical Module** (Dedicated live streaming & burst snapshot camera)

### Actuator Circuit Details

#### 1. Haptic Vibration Motor (GPIO 12)
Because vibration motors draw up to 80-120mA and generate inductive back-EMF:
- **Switch:** Logic-level N-Channel MOSFET (e.g. IRLML2502 or 2N7000)
- **Gate Resistor:** 100Ω between GPIO 12 and Gate
- **Pull-down:** 10kΩ between Gate and GND (prevents motor spinning during ESP32 boot)
- **Flyback Diode:** 1N4148 or 1N5819 connected in parallel across the motor terminals (cathode to VCC, anode to drain) to suppress inductive voltage spikes.

#### 2. Piezo Buzzer (GPIO 14)
- **Transistor:** 2N2222 or BC547 NPN
- **Base Resistor:** 1kΩ from GPIO 14 to Base
- **Collector:** Connected to Buzzer negative pin; Buzzer positive pin connected to 3.3V or 5V.

#### 3. Battery Voltage Divider (GPIO 34)
The ESP32 ADC accepts a maximum of 3.3V. A single-cell LiPo battery charges up to 4.20V.
- $R_1 = 100\text{ k}\Omega$ (between LiPo positive terminal and GPIO 34)
- $R_2 = 100\text{ k}\Omega$ (between GPIO 34 and GND)
- Divider Ratio: $V_{\text{ADC}} = V_{\text{BAT}} \times \frac{100}{100 + 100} = 0.5 \times V_{\text{BAT}}$
- Maximum voltage at GPIO 34 for 4.2V is $2.10\text{ V}$, well within the linear range of ESP32 ADC attenuation (`ADC_11db`).
- Filter capacitor: 100nF ceramic cap between GPIO 34 and GND suppresses ADC noise.

### Power Supply Requirements for ESP32-CAM
- The ESP32-CAM draws up to **310-450 mA during Wi-Fi transmission and JPEG compression bursts**.
- Power input: **5V on the 5V pin** (regulated onboard to 3.3V via AMS1117-3.3).
- **CRITICAL:** Do NOT attempt to power the ESP32-CAM from USB 3.3V FTDI programmers directly, as voltage brownout resets (`Brownout detector was triggered`) will occur during camera frame capture.
- A $470\mu\text{F}$ electrolytic capacitor placed across the 5V and GND pins is strongly recommended.
